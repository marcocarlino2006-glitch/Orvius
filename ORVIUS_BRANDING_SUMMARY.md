## New Logo Design

### Logo Components
The new ORVIUS logo consists of two distinct parts:

1. **Minimal Square Mark (Icon)**
   - Black rounded-corner square
   - Centered thin orange circle (concentric design)
   - Creates an aperture or portal-like effect
   - Minimal, modern, geometric aesthetic

2. **Tracked Wordmark**
   - "ORVIUS" in all-caps sans-serif typeface
   - Significant letter-spacing (tracking)
   - Clean, modern, architectural feel
   - Positioned to the right of the square mark

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Black (#000000 or similar dark tone)
- **Accent:** Vibrant orange (used in logo circle, phone number, primary CTA)
- **Neutrals:** White, off-white backgrounds, light gray text

---

## Typography System

### Primary Characteristics
- **Font Family:** Modern, clean sans-serif (likely a geometric or grotesque typeface)
- **Case Style:** Heavy emphasis on **UPPERCASE typography** throughout
- **Letter-spacing:** Generous tracking on headlines and wordmark
- **Hierarchy:** Established through size and weight variations

### Typography Applications

1. **Headlines**
   - All-caps, bold weight
   - Significant size contrast
   - Example: "CALL THE LIVE LINE."
   - Example: "INBOX, JOBS, DISPATCH, ASK — ONE SYSTEM OF RECORD."

2. **Labels & Categories**
   - Small all-caps text
   - Light gray color
   - Example: "PRODUCTION LINE · SUMMIT HVAC"
   - Example: "PLATFORM"

3. **Body Text**
   - Regular weight sans-serif
   - Gray color for secondary information
   - Normal sentence case for descriptive paragraphs

4. **Emphasis Elements**
   - Phone number in large orange type
   - Bold weight for subheadlines
   - White text on dark backgrounds for contrast

---

## Design Philosophy
The new ORVIUS branding conveys:
- **Professionalism:** Clean, architectural typography
- **Modernity:** Minimal geometric logo, generous spacing
- **Authority:** Bold uppercase headlines
- **Focus:** High contrast with vibrant orange accents
- **Clarity:** Simple, uncluttered visual hierarchy

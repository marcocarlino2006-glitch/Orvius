# What multi-billion-dollar AI software actually sells

Valuation is a financing mark, not proof of durable product value. Still, the
companies below reveal a consistent product pattern that Orvius can use without
copying their market or their branding.

## The shared mechanics

1. **Enter through one expensive job.** They replace labor or unlock revenue;
   they do not begin as a general chatbot.
2. **Own the workflow, not the answer.** Context enters, the agent acts in the
   system of record, and a business outcome comes out.
3. **Prove every action.** Sources, timestamps, status and audit history make
   autonomy trustworthy.
4. **Escalate exceptions.** Routine work runs automatically; high-risk work
   waits for a human decision.
5. **Tie price to value.** Resolutions, completed work and revenue outcomes
   align spend with customer benefit better than seats.
6. **Expand from the wedge.** Abridge moved from notes toward reimbursement;
   Sierra moved from support toward the customer lifecycle; Harvey expanded
   from assistance into long-running legal workflows.

## Orvius translation

Orvius's wedge is the missed or after-hours service call. The complete unit of
value is not a transcript. It is:

```text
call → qualified demand → booked job → dispatched technician → collected money
```

The product should therefore optimize and report:

- qualified calls captured;
- jobs booked from Orvius-captured demand;
- response and booking rate;
- estimated value only when the shop supplied an average ticket;
- deposits and payments actually collected;
- exceptions requiring owner approval;
- every agent action, its status and its evidence.

## Product rules

- Never label estimated pipeline as recovered or collected revenue.
- Never hide an agent action after execution or dismissal.
- Never let a high-risk customer, schedule or money action bypass approval
  until the shop explicitly delegates that procedure.
- Never make the owner reconstruct outcomes from separate CRM pages.
- Do not add a generic chatbot where a defined action can finish the work.

## Sequence

1. **Outcome command:** lead with measured work completed and owner exceptions.
2. **Agent control:** approvals plus a tenant-scoped execution audit trail.
3. **Reliable procedures:** formalize booking, follow-up, assignment and deposit
   collection as versioned agent operating procedures.
4. **Closed-loop economics:** connect each captured call to booking, dispatch and
   collected money.
5. **Outcome pricing:** charge against clearly agreed completed outcomes only
   after measurement and reliability are proven.
6. **System expansion:** customer history, technician operations, payments and
   supplier workflows grow from the same demand record.

The first two steps are now represented on Command. The next moat is not more
dashboard decoration; it is reliable execution across steps three and four.

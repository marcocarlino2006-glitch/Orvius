## Formula (do not reverse)

1. **Own a painful workflow** — HVAC after-hours/overflow receptionist  
2. **Accumulate proprietary operational data** — every call, job, outcome structured  
3. **Become the system of record** — one shop brain for demand → work → money  
4. **Control actions and transactions** — bookings, alerts, later payments  
5. **Expand adjacent industries** — only after HVAC wedge is undefeated  

Intelligence (OpenAI / Google / Meta models) is **replaceable**.  
Orvius value = **context · workflow · permissions · data · transaction layer**.

## What we never skip

| Step | Why |
|------|-----|
| One local HVAC shop on overflow/after-hours | Distribution + trust |

| Call → cash measurement | Demand → completed work → money |

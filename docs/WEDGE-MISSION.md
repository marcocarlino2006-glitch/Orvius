# Orvius wedge mission (founder lock)

**Locked:** 2026-09-22  
**Source of truth in code:** `src/lib/company.ts` (`mission`, `vision`, `strategy`, `categoryClaim`, `proofLine`)

## Beginning (what we are now)

Orvius begins as a **focused AI receptionist for HVAC companies** that:

- answers inbound calls
- understands the customer’s problem
- captures service address and contact information
- identifies urgency
- checks the service area
- books an appointment
- sends confirmations
- notifies the business
- escalates uncertain situations to a human

**First deployment:** one local HVAC company, preferably **overflow or after-hours**.  
**Commercial:** charge for a **controlled pilot**.  
**Prove:** Orvius turns calls that would have been missed into **real profitable work**.

Track: calls answered · leads captured · appointments booked · jobs completed · revenue influenced.

## What the beginning is not

Not building the entire future OS up front.  
Not dispatch, tech workflows, equipment history, payments, or the broader platform — **until** one business pays and gets measurable value.

## Expansion order (only after the wedge pays)

1. Repeat with several HVAC companies  
2. Add missed-call recovery, follow-up, estimates, memberships, revenue analytics  
3. **Then** expand into dispatch, technician workflows, equipment history, payments, and the broader operating system  

## The one-sentence goal

Prove that **one HVAC company will pay Orvius to turn one customer call into one completed and paid job.**

## Dashboard north star (Operate)

**Main question:** How much demand did Orvius capture, how much became completed work, and how much money did that work produce?

### Call → cash path (show the complete path)

### Activity feed (every call)

### Owner top-line cards

### Orvius ops cards

AI accuracy · booking errors · escalation rate · response time · system uptime · customer retention.

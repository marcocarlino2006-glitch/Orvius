# Launch post package

The Manus/Cursor lesson: don't announce features — post a moment of the
impossible thing working, make it tryable in one tap, and let scarcity do
the distribution. Our moment is stronger than a demo video: a live phone
number anyone can call, answered as a real shop, booking a real slot.

Post ONLY after the portal sequence is done (docs/OPEN-THE-PORTAL.md).
A viral post into a closed signup is a wasted wave.

## The hero asset (film this first)

A single screen recording, under 60 seconds, no voiceover needed:
1. You dial +1 844 643 9170 on camera (or show the browser demo at /try).
2. The receptionist answers as the demo shop; you ask for a same-day AC
   repair with a messy, real-customer request.
3. Cut to the owner's phone: the alert text. Cut to Command: the job on
   the schedule, assigned.
4. End card: "This call was answered by Orvius. Call it yourself."

Captions burned in. Post the raw recording, not a polished ad — the
category believes raw.

## X / Twitter (launch day, founder voice)

Post 1 (the moment):
> This is a real HVAC shop's phone line at 11 PM.
> Nobody's there. Watch what happens.
> [video]
> Then call it yourself: +1 844 643 9170

Post 2 (the wedge):
> Every night, trade shops miss calls they already paid to generate.
> Each one is a $400 job going to the next name on Google.
> Orvius answers in the shop's name, books it on the real calendar,
> texts the customer, and shows the owner the proof.

Post 3 (the claim mechanic):
> Own a shop? Claim your front door:
> orvius.im/launch — type your shop's name, hear it answer as you,
> send the call to another owner.

Post 4 (scarcity, only if capacity is set):
> October founding cohort: [X] shops. When a metro fills, we stop
> onboarding there and win it properly. [density line if true]

Rules: real numbers only, ever. No "revolutionary", no "10x", no
metrics we don't have. The honesty is the brand voice.

## LinkedIn (same day, story version)

Lead with the missed-call economics of one night, the 60-second video,
and the claim link. End with what we're looking for: HVAC, plumbing and
electrical owners in [first metro] for the founding cohort.

## Trade communities (Facebook groups, Reddit, forums)

No launch language. Post the video as a working owner would:
"Set this up to answer our after-hours line — recorded a test call,
what do you think?" Answer every comment. One thread, genuinely
responded to, outperforms ten announcements.

## What success looks like in the first 48 hours

Measured on the admin board, not in likes:
- Previews started (hear-your-shop) spiking
- Previews that took a call
- Replays shared and viewed — the viral coefficient, finally real
- Claims turning into signups inside the cohort capacity

## Never do

- Post before signup is open.
- Buy ads against an unproven booking rate.
- Announce the Network, the endpoint, or the $100B thesis publicly.
  The market should discover the moat by running into it.

# Homepage structure study — category leaders

Individual breakdowns for Orvius reference. No implementation — research only.

---

## 1. Anthropic (anthropic.com)

**Category feel:** Research lab / essay, not a product marketing site.

**First viewport**
- Almost no product UI chrome
- One philosophical statement: “Anthropic is built on hard questions.”
- Soft CTA: “Learn more”
- No feature grid, no pricing, no fake stats in hero

**Page rhythm**
1. Mission/philosophy statement
2. Latest releases (editorial cards — Opus, Sonnet, announcements)
3. Second mission line: “we build AI to serve humanity’s long-term well-being”
4. Deep links into safety / policy / research / education

**What makes it billion-dollar**
- Confidence without selling hard
- Type and whitespace do the work
- Content feels like a publication, not a funnel
- Brand = trust + seriousness

**Orvius takeaway**
- Hero can be a single serious statement + one CTA
- Avoid “feature dump” energy
- Mission and product should feel inevitable, not pitchy

---

## 2. Claude product surface (claude.ai / claude.com)

**Category feel:** Product is the brand. Calm utility.

**Structure pattern**
- Minimal header (logo + account/profile)
- Product workspace is center stage
- Profile / account control consolidates settings, projects, account
- Soft panels, restrained color, editorial type
- Popup/menu pulls navigation together — not a fat top nav

**What makes it billion-dollar**
- Feels finished and quiet
- One control surface for “everything”
- No marketing noise inside the product

**Orvius takeaway**
- Keep the profile button + popup as the control center
- Marketing site and product shell should share the same calm language
- Homepage sells; product doesn’t scream

---

## 3. Stripe (stripe.com)

**Category feel:** Global financial infrastructure.

**First viewport**
- One category claim: “Financial infrastructure to grow your revenue.”
- Short supporting sentence (payments → billionth transaction)
- Two CTAs: Get started / Sign up with Google
- No photo hero required — authority from words + clarity

**Page rhythm**
1. Category definition
2. Flexible solutions (product pillars)
3. Proof scale ($1.9T, uptime, currencies) — *earned* metrics
4. Customer stories (named companies)
5. Developer / integration depth
6. Closing CTA

**What makes it billion-dollar**
- Speaks like the default layer of the internet economy
- Proof only after the claim is clear
- Product breadth shown as infrastructure, not a tool list

**Orvius takeaway**
- Lead with category: “AI operating partner for service businesses”
- Show wedge (calls/leads) as the first proof of that category
- Don’t fake Stripe-scale metrics — earn them later

---

## 4. Linear (linear.app)

**Category feel:** Craft + speed + product system.

**First viewport**
- One line: “The product development system for teams and agents”
- Short support: purpose-built / AI era
- Product UI is the hero (living interface, not stock photo)

**Page rhythm**
1. Category claim
2. Product demo embedded in scroll (intake, planning, AI, ship)
3. Principles (purpose-built / agents / speed)
4. Social proof quotes
5. Closing: “Built for the future. Available today.”

**What makes it billion-dollar**
- The product *is* the marketing
- Motion and UI craft signal quality
- Short copy, high precision

**Orvius takeaway**
- Owner-alert / product surface can stand in for “demo”
- Prefer one living product moment over galleries of screenshots
- End with calm inevitability, not hype

---

## 5. Apple (apple.com)

**Category feel:** Cultural + product theater.

**First viewport**
- Huge product statement / event
- Minimal text, maximum product presence
- Learn more / Shop — never a cluttered form in hero

**Page rhythm**
- Stacked full-bleed product chapters (iPhone, Mac, Watch…)
- Each section: one product, one line, two links
- Extreme consistency

**What makes it billion-dollar**
- Ruthless simplicity
- One idea per viewport
- Brand never explains too much

**Orvius takeaway**
- One idea per section — enforced
- Don’t mix pilot form, features, and vision in the same band
- Let silence sell quality

---

## 6. Notion (notion.com)

**Category feel:** System of record / workspace OS.

**First viewport pattern**
- Short command-like claim: “Bring everything into one system of record.”
- Product implied as the destination
- Sparse intro → expands into use cases

**What makes it billion-dollar**
- Sounds like infrastructure for work
- “System of record” language = platform destiny
- Clean, product-forward

**Orvius takeaway**
- Long-term language: operating partner / system for the business
- Day-one wedge still concrete (calls/leads)
- Homepage can hold both: category + wedge

---

## 7. Vercel (vercel.com)

**Category feel:** Modern infrastructure for builders/agents.

**First viewport**
- Bold category: “Agentic Infrastructure”
- One sentence of purpose
- Two CTAs: Deploy / Talk to sales

**Page rhythm**
1. Category claim
2. Customer case chapters (Notion, Zapier, Mintlify) — each with one idea
3. Recently shipped
4. Closing CTA

**What makes it billion-dollar**
- Speaks to the future category they want to own
- Customer proof as chapters, not logo salad in hero
- Developer-grade clarity

**Orvius takeaway**
- Category claim first
- Customer chapters later (after real pilots)
- Dual CTA ok: Pilot + Product

## Cross-company pattern (what they share)

| Pattern | How they do it |
|--------|----------------|
| **Category in one line** | Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Notion |
| **Sparse hero** | Anthropic, Apple, Claude |
| **One idea per section** | All of them |
| **Product as proof** | Linear, Stripe, Claude |
| **No fake hero stats** | Anthropic / Claude especially |
| **Calm type + space** | Anthropic, Linear, Apple |
| **Dark/light band rhythm** | Anthropic, Stripe closing CTAs |
| **Profile/account consolidates product** | Claude app |

## What Orvius should copy (and not copy)

**Copy**
- Anthropic/Claude: restraint, editorial type, quiet confidence
- Stripe: category sentence + wedge explanation
- Linear: product surface as the visual proof (no stock photos)
- Apple: one job per section
- Claude app: profile popup as the “everything” control

**Don’t copy yet**
- Stripe’s trillion-dollar metrics (we haven’t earned them)
- Apple’s product photography machine
- Vercel’s dense customer case stack (need real customers first)

## Recommended Orvius homepage blueprint (from this study)

1. **Nav** — Orvius · Product · Start free pilot  
2. **Hero** — Category + wedge in 2 lines, one CTA (no images)  
3. **Statement** — Why this matters (missed calls vs AI ops)  
4. **How** — 3 steps, text only  
5. **Product** — Owner alert / living UI surface  
6. **Close** — Pilot CTA on dark band  
7. **Product shell** — Profile button + popup everywhere inside app  

This is the multi-billion structure without lying about scale.

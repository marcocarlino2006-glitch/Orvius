# The front-office feed

Decision, 2026-10-18: Command is a feed an owner supervises, not software
he operates. The signed-in home reads in the order an owner thinks:

1. **Direct** — one bar: ask Orvius to book, move, assign, or check work.
2. **Needs you** — the few things only a person can decide, approvals first.
3. **What Orvius did** — the last 24 hours, each action with its result.
4. **Detail below** — counts, tomorrow, money, schedule, records. One tap
   down, never the headline.

The depth (Jobs, Schedule, Customers, Team, Reports, Price book) does not
shrink; it stops being the front door. A solo owner can live in the feed.
A crew grows into the depth.

## Build rule

Frozen: no new pages, sections, or settings unless a real shop is blocked
without them. Every proposed change answers one question first —

> Does this make the owner feel like he hired an employee, or like he
> bought software?

Employee wins. Software waits.

## Why

The buyer is an owner between jobs, on a phone, for 40 seconds. The pitch
is "we do the work"; a home he must operate contradicts it. Incumbents own
the dashboard paradigm; the supervised feed is the ground where an
AI-first front office is structurally ahead.

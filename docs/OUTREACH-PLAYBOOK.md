# First 10 pilots — Orvius outreach playbook

**Goal:** 10 home-service businesses on a free 30-day pilot.

**Wedge:** Never miss a call. Orvius answers, qualifies, books, and texts you a summary.

**Domain:** orvius.im · Pilot page: `/pilot` · Demo: `/demo`

---

## Ideal customer

- HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing
- 2–15 person crew
- Owner answers (or misses) most calls themselves
- No full-time receptionist
- High inbound call volume / after-hours jobs

---

## Daily outreach targets

| Channel | Volume / day | Notes |
|---------|--------------|-------|
| Google Maps DMs / calls | 20 | Search “[trade] near me” |
| Facebook trade groups | 10 comments/DMs | Local contractor groups |
| Warm intros | 5 | Friends, family, past vendors |
| **Total** | **~35 touches** | Expect 1–2 conversations that go deep |

---

## Cold call script (30 seconds)

> Hey [Name], this is [You] with Orvius. Quick question — when you're on a job and a call comes in after hours, what happens to that lead right now?
>
> *(Let them talk.)*
>
> We built an AI receptionist for HVAC/plumbing shops that answers every call, qualifies it, and texts you the summary so you don't lose jobs. I'm looking for 10 shops for a free 30-day pilot — I do the setup myself. Open to a 10-minute walkthrough this week?

---

## Text / DM script

> Hey [Name] — saw [Business] on Google. Curious: how many calls do you miss after hours or while you're on a job?
>
> We're piloting Orvius — AI that answers, books, and texts you the lead. Free for 30 days, I set it up personally for the first 10 shops.
>
> Want me to show a 2-min demo? → orvius.im/demo

---

## Follow-up (if they say maybe)

> Quick follow-up — one saved emergency job usually covers months of Orvius. Still open to a free pilot this week? I can walk you through the demo in 10 minutes.

---

## Demo call flow (use /demo)

1. Open **orvius.im/demo** (or current tunnel URL)
2. Pick “AC emergency” preset → run simulation
3. Show **dashboard** — lead + summary appear
4. Explain: live version uses their Twilio number + Vapi; same result on real calls
5. Close: “Want to be one of the 10? I’ll onboard you this week.”

---

## Close + onboard checklist

- [ ] Get business name, owner phone, business phone
- [ ] Add them in `/admin` once Twilio/Vapi keys are live
- [ ] Connect number in Vapi
- [ ] Place test call together
- [ ] Confirm owner SMS works
- [ ] Check in Day 3 and Day 7

---

## Success metric for each pilot

**% of inbound leads captured vs. before Orvius**

If you can't measure that, you're not proving the product.
